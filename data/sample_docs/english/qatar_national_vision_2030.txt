Qatar National Vision 2030 (QNV 2030) is a comprehensive development plan launched in October 2008 by the General Secretariat for Development Planning in the State of Qatar. The aim of QNV 2030 is to transform Qatar into an advanced society capable of achieving sustainable development and securing a high standard of living for its people for generations to come.

The Vision defines long-term outcomes for the country and provides a framework within which national strategies and implementation plans can be developed.

The Four Key Pillars of Qatar National Vision 2030:
1. Human Development: Development of all Qatar's people to enable them to sustain a prosperous society. This emphasizes world-class education, comprehensive healthcare, and effective cultural and labor programs.
2. Social Development: Development of a just and caring society based on high moral standards and social capabilities, capable of playing a significant role in global partnerships.
3. Economic Development: Development of a competitive and diversified economy capable of meeting the needs of, and securing a high standard of living for, all its people both for the present and for the future, balancing between oil and gas assets and knowledge-based economic expansion.
4. Environmental Development: Management of the environment such that there is harmony between economic growth, social development, and environmental protection. This includes strict emissions standards, biodiversity conservation, and green infrastructure investments.
